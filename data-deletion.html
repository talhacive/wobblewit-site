Data deletion

Wobble Wit Studio

Wobble Wit Studio stores data only on the creator's own computer (see the privacy policy).

If you commented on a Wobble Wit video

Delete your comment on the platform; the next time the studio counts votes it will no longer be counted. To have a stored copy removed straight away, send a message to the Wobble Wit Facebook Page at facebook.com/wobblewit with a link to the comment. It will be deleted within 7 days.

Disconnecting accounts

The creator can disconnect any platform in the studio's settings, which deletes its sign-in tokens from the computer's keychain, and can also remove the app's access in each platform's own account settings.
