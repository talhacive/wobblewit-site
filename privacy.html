Privacy policy

Wobble Wit Studio · last updated 15 November 2026

What the app is

Wobble Wit Studio is a private application used only by the creator of Wobble Wit to publish their own videos to their own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, and to read the results of those posts. It runs on the creator's own computer. It is not offered to the public and no other person signs in to it.

What data it uses

  • Account basics of the creator's own accounts (account or channel ID, name, username), to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The creator's own posts: uploading videos and their titles and captions, and reading each post's public counts (views, likes, comments).
  • Public comments on the creator's own posts, read only to count audience votes (for example "ASK" or "MIDWIFE") that decide what the next part of a story is. Commenters' names are not stored; the app keeps the comment text and a one-way hashed identifier only to avoid counting the same person twice.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the creator's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored in the computer's encrypted keychain. Post statistics and comment text are stored in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is uploaded to any other server, except the videos and captions the creator chooses to publish on the platforms above.

Sharing and selling

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. No advertising or tracking is used, and this website sets no cookies.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept only while it is useful for the creator's own analysis. See Data deletion for how to have anything removed.
